Leaving mid-November, back in January: prices on 13 October 2026

Every departure from 10 to 20 November was priced again on 13 October 2026 against six return dates in January, 66 date pairs in all. The search is the same as September's: one adult in economy, return ticket, US airports excluded, and anything over 36 hours or with three stops left out. The rest of this page still shows the 11 September prices.

Qantas and LATAM cost A$3,779 to A$4,885. The return date sets the price here: coming back on 31 January costs A$3,779 to A$4,118, and every earlier return sampled costs A$4,071 to A$4,885. The departure day makes little difference: for any one return date the eleven departure days sit within about A$550 of each other. The routing now runs through Sydney or Melbourne and then Santiago, not Auckland, and 59 of the 66 cheapest fares include a flight operated by Wamos for LATAM. Google showed a fare without one on 41 of those 59 pairs: on 35 it cost A$30 to A$1,660 more, and on the other six it was a far dearer routing through Auckland.

Air Canada is still the cheapest at A$2,119 to A$3,992, and still needs the Canadian visa.

Qatar Airways is new. In September it was not sold as a return ticket. It now is, through DOH with one stop and 33 h 30 on the way out. It showed a fare on three of the eleven departures (Tuesday 10, Saturday 14 and Tuesday 17 November) and two of the six returns (Wednesday 6 and Sunday 31 January): A$3,582 to A$3,623 coming back on 31 January, which is A$188 to A$536 under Qantas and LATAM on the same dates, and A$4,441 to A$4,483 coming back on 6 January. The other four return dates fall on days with no Qatar fare, so its other January returns are not priced yet. Also not checked yet: the transit rules at Doha for a Colombian passport, and the routing of the flight back.

Against September. One pair was priced both times, 17 November to 16 January: Qantas and LATAM went from A$4,927 to A$4,233, and Air Canada from A$2,926 to A$3,505.

What the 11 September search found

Brisbane to Bogotá and back, one adult in economy, two or three months in Colombia, leaving any time from November 2026 to March 2027, never touching the USA. Google Flights was searched with every US airport removed from its connecting-airports filter: departures every four days from 1 November to 31 March, each with a return about 60 days and about 90 days later, plus extra pairs around Christmas and around the cheapest dates. One-way fares in each direction were sampled for the same dates so a return fare can be set against them.

Only two airlines sell a non-US through-ticket from Brisbane. A Sydney to Santiago through-fare appeared on one date only (5 November), and nothing via the Middle East, Madrid or Mexico City is sold as a single return ticket.

Reading the prices. The departure date sets the price and the return date barely moves it. On the Qantas and LATAM routing, Christmas in Colombia (leaving 1 December) costs about A$750 more than a mid-January departure, and leaving in the week before Christmas roughly doubles the fare. If Christmas matters, book the 1 December departure rather than anything later that month. November is no cheaper than early December on this routing: the fares found were A$3,540 to A$5,285, against A$3,585 for 1 December. Air Canada is cheap in November, but the Canadian visa problem stands. A two-month stay does not save money: Qantas and LATAM showed no through-fare at all on most two-month pairs before February, and where both lengths were priced the fares match to within a few dollars (about A$2,700 for a March departure either way). The two-month Christmas plan has no Qantas and LATAM fare in the sample; Air Canada priced it at A$3,506 to A$3,556 for a 3 or 7 December departure. A return ticket beats two one-ways on 97 of the 102 pairs where both were priced, usually by A$700 to A$2,200. The exceptions are the pre-Christmas Qantas and LATAM departures (15 and 19 December), where the return fare is so inflated that two one-ways come in A$2,000 to A$3,500 cheaper, at about A$5,500 to A$6,100: still far above the 1 December return. One-way fares do open a third non-US routing, Qatar Airways via Doha, at about A$2,300 to A$2,600 outbound and A$3,200 back with Iberia via Madrid, but it is not sold as a return and still costs more than the Qantas and LATAM return.

To confirm before booking: the checked-bag allowance on the fare shown; which passport to travel on, since the Australian visa is tied to one and the transit rules differ by passport; the NZeTA if the Auckland routing is chosen; and that the Santiago to Bogotá leg is on LATAM or Avianca, not the Wamos wet-lease Google flagged on some December dates.
